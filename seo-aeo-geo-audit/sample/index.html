Executive Summary (Sample)

The company has a strong 20-year local reputation and steady referral work, yet its website currently hides what it does best behind a generic template. Search engines only index one catch-all services page, and AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity) cite two smaller competitors first because those competitors publish clear service pages, structured local data, and direct answers to buyer questions.

1. Design, Messaging & Conversion

A first-time visitor arriving on mobile takes nearly ten seconds to confirm which commercial services the company actually handles and which counties it covers. The primary inquiry form asks nine required questions on a separate page, causing unnecessary drop-off for quick estimate requests.

  • Headline fix: Replace "Quality Service You Can Trust Since 1998" with a clear service-and-location statement (e.g., "Commercial HVAC & Mechanical Service Across Southeast Michigan").
  • Proof above the fold: Surface licensing, response-time commitments, and review count in a compact credentials strip right below the hero.
  • Two conversion paths: Cut the quote form to four fields and add a secondary direct scheduling or callback link for urgent commercial buyers.

2. Technical SEO & Crawl Hygiene

The site loads cleanly over HTTPS and has no crawl blocks, which is a good baseline. However, three structural issues hold back local rankings:

  • One URL targeting five services: Google rarely ranks a single generic /services/ page for distinct commercial queries. Splitting out dedicated pages with focused title tags (≤60 chars) and meta descriptions (≤155 chars) creates clean ranking targets.
  • Mobile Largest Contentful Paint (LCP): An uncompressed 4.2 MB stock banner pushes mobile load past 5 seconds. Converting to properly sized WebP/AVIF with fetchpriority="high" cuts load time in half.
  • Missing local area signals: Footer and contact pages mention only one town, leaving surrounding service counties invisible to regional search queries.

3. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)

Google AI Overviews and voice/answer tools pull concise, self-contained explanations. Right now, none of the site's headings are framed around the questions buyers actually ask before calling (emergency response times, preventative maintenance contracts, equipment brands serviced, or service territory).

  • Add direct 40 to 60 word Q&A blocks: Place a clean accordion FAQ on the homepage and each core service page answering buyer-intent questions directly in the first sentence.
  • Ship a linked JSON-LD graph: Connect WebSite, LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQPage nodes via @id so crawlers verify your exact service area, phone number, and offerings without guessing.

4. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)

I tested four realistic buyer prompts across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Google AI Overviews (for example: "Who are the most reliable commercial mechanical contractors near Monroe and Downriver Michigan?").

How to fix GEO standing: Publish the exact same one-sentence company description across your website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn Company Page, and local chamber profiles; confirm robots.txt allows GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot; and add a clean /llms.txt index pointing directly to your core service pages.

Prioritized 30-Day Action Plan

  1. Week 1 (Quick Wins): Fix homepage title/meta/H1, compress hero images, shorten the contact form, and verify robots.txt + sitemap.xml in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
  2. Weeks 2 to 3 (Core Architecture): Publish dedicated service pages with 4-question buyer FAQs, deploy linked Schema.org JSON-LD + llms.txt, and align Google Business Profile service areas.
  3. Week 4+ (Ongoing Proof): Request 5 recent client Google reviews mentioning specific services performed, and publish one practical buyer guide per month targeting real pre-sale questions.
