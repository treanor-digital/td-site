App Support & FAQ

Need a hand with ASE Prep?

Questions about restoring a purchase, reporting a technical question issue, or getting notified when new exam banks go live? Reach the developer directly.

How do I restore purchases on a new iPhone or iPad?

Open the Study Hub tab, tap the Unlock or Master Bundle button in the top right, and select Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account used for your original purchase. StoreKit 2 verifies your active entitlements automatically.

Is this a recurring subscription?

No. Every unlock is a one-time lifetime purchase ($9.99 for a single exam or $39.99 for all nine A1 through A9 exams). If you unlock a single exam first, you receive an automatic $10 credit if you later upgrade to the Master Tech Bundle ($29.99 upgrade).

Does ASE Prep work offline in the shop?

Yes. All question banks, timed exam simulations, explanations, shop reference tables, and study progress are stored locally on your device so you can study without Wi-Fi or cell signal.

How is my privacy handled and how do I delete my data?

ASE Prep requires no account, runs zero ads, and includes no third-party tracking SDKs. All study progress stays on your device. Deleting the app removes all local study data. Read our full ASE Prep Privacy Addendum.
