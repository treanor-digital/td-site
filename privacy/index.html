This policy explains what personal information Treanor Digital LLC collects on treanordigital.com, how we use it, and how we handle information in mobile applications we publish under our own name. If any sentence here is unclear, email us and we will explain it directly.

1. Information this website collects

We collect very little on this website. There is no account to create, nothing to purchase on the website, and no advertising or behavioral tracking. The practices below reflect what this site actually runs.

Contact, Free Analysis & App Support forms

Forms on treanordigital.com (including our homepage inquiry form, our Free Site & AI Search Report form, and our app support form) are processed by Netlify Forms, the forms feature of our web host, Netlify. Depending on which form you submit, we receive:

  • Full name (required)
  • Work or contact email address (required)
  • Company name (optional on the main inquiry form)
  • Website URL (optional on the main inquiry form, for a Free Site & AI Search Report)
  • Area of interest (your selected inquiry type or Free Site & AI Search Report)
  • Target timeline and estimated budget (optional fields shown for build sprint or advisory inquiries)
  • Your message or app version details (required)

Each form also includes a hidden honeypot field intended solely to filter automated spam submissions. Please leave it blank. We do not use it to collect information about you.

If you submit a website URL for a Free Site & AI Search Report (design, copy, SEO, AEO, and GEO review), we use that URL solely to prepare your private report. We do not publish your report, share your audit findings with third parties, or add you to a mailing list.

Netlify stores form submissions inside our Netlify account so we can read and reply to them, attaching standard technical metadata such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and timestamp. Details are covered in the Netlify Privacy Policy.

Hosting logs

When your browser requests a page or file, Netlify records standard server logs (IP address, date and time, requested path, browser type, and referring URL if sent). Logs are used to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the site. We do not install third-party web analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or tag managers.

Fonts

All website typefaces (Cormorant Garamond, Inter, and IBM Plex Mono) are self-hosted directly on treanordigital.com. Your browser does not make third-party requests to Google Fonts or any external font CDN when loading pages.

Cookies, storage & external links

This website does not set tracking or advertising cookies, and its JavaScript does not store persistent identifiers in local storage or session storage to track you across visits. Outbound links (such as our LinkedIn profile, Google Calendar appointment booking page, or the Apple App Store) are ordinary links rather than embedded tracking widgets; once you follow a link, that destination's privacy policy applies.

2. How we use information, and when we share it

We use personal information solely to:

  • Respond to your inquiry, prepare a requested analysis report, or answer an app support question
  • Operate, secure, and troubleshoot treanordigital.com
  • Deliver and maintain the mobile applications we publish
  • Comply with legal obligations or respond to lawful requests

We share website information only with our web host (Netlify for hosting and form processing), or with a public authority if required by law. We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or pass it to data brokers.

3. Mobile applications (General policy)

This section covers mobile applications published directly by Treanor Digital LLC on the Apple App Store and Google Play. It does not cover apps or websites built for consulting clients; when a client publishes its own product, that client's privacy policy governs that product.

Our apps collect only what is strictly necessary to function. Depending on the app, that may include:

  • Account details you provide (only if a future app offers account creation)
  • In-app content or preferences saved so the feature works as expected
  • Platform diagnostics (such as OS crash reports opted into through Apple or Google)
  • Purchase entitlement status handled natively by Apple StoreKit or Google Play Billing. Apple or Google processes all payments; Treanor Digital LLC never receives or stores your payment card details.

We do not sell app data, integrate third-party advertising networks, or track users across other companies' apps and websites. Platform-level handling is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

4. App-Specific Addendum: ASE A-Series Certification Prep

Summary for Apple App Store & Google Play

ASE A-Series Certification Prep is built offline-first with privacy by default. We do not collect, transmit, or store personal data on external Treanor Digital servers.

  • No account required: The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or login credentials.
  • On-device storage only: Your selected exam, question mastery progress, bookmarked items, and mock exam score history are stored locally on your device via Apple's standard UserDefaults storage. That data never leaves your device unless backed up by your own iCloud or device backup settings.
  • Payments via Apple StoreKit 2 (and Google Play Billing on Android): Optional one-time exam unlocks and the Master Tech Bundle are processed exclusively by Apple (or Google). The app verifies purchase entitlements locally through platform APIs; Treanor Digital LLC does not collect credit card numbers, billing addresses, or Apple IDs.
  • Zero advertising or third-party tracking SDKs: The app contains no ad networks, no behavioral analytics trackers, and no cross-app tracking.
  • Deleting your data: Because all study progress lives locally on your device, resetting progress in the app or uninstalling the app permanently removes your local study data. If you email us for app support, see Your rights & deletion below.

5. Retention & security

We retain website inquiry and support emails only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, follow up, and maintain ordinary business communication records. You can request deletion at any time and we will delete your messages within 30 days unless a legal duty requires retention.

All pages and form submissions on treanordigital.com are transmitted over HTTPS encryption. Access to form submissions and support messages is restricted to principal leadership. While we apply sensible safeguards for a small firm, no online transmission or storage method can be guaranteed 100% secure.

6. Children's privacy

This website and our published mobile applications (including ASE A-Series Certification Prep, built for automotive technicians and vocational learners) are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information to us, email kevin.treanor@treanordigital.com and we will delete it promptly. This serves as our notice under the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

7. International users

Treanor Digital LLC operates from Monroe, Michigan, USA. If you visit our website or contact us from outside the United States, your information is processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from your local jurisdiction. We have not appointed a formal EU or UK privacy representative; however, we honor access, correction, and deletion requests from users worldwide as described below.

8. Your rights & data deletion requests

You may email us at any time to request that we:

  • Confirm whether we hold personal information about you
  • Provide a copy of the personal information we hold
  • Correct inaccurate details
  • Delete your personal information, inquiry history, or any app account (for future apps that support accounts)

Send requests to kevin.treanor@treanordigital.com from the email address associated with your inquiry or app account. We may ask to verify your identity before acting and will complete your request within 30 days. Exercising these rights will never result in reduced service or discrimination.

9. California residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you reside in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA) provides the rights described in Section 8 along with clear disclosure of the categories collected on this website:

  • Identifiers: Name, email address, and IP address from form submissions and hosting logs.
  • Professional information: Company name and project or website details you choose to share.
  • Internet or network activity: Requested pages, timestamps, and referring headers in standard host logs.

We do not collect sensitive personal information (as defined by the CPRA), precise geolocation, or government IDs on this website. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Because we do not sell or share personal data, no separate "Do Not Sell or Share" opt-out link is required, though you may always email kevin.treanor@treanordigital.com to direct us directly or via an authorized agent.
