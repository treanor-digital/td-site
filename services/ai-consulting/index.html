Why Most Small Business AI Pilots Stall

Most growing companies do not have an AI technology problem. They have an implementation problem. Leadership buys seats to three chat subscriptions, teams run a few experiments, and six weeks later everyone slides back to old habits because nobody wired AI into real workflows or wrote down clear safety rules.

My approach starts from your actual work, not model hype. Before writing any code, I distinguish between two very different goals: augmenting your core craft (sharpening client deliverables, research, and analysis) versus automating operational overhead (clearing inbox triage, reporting prep, and repetitive admin work).

What I Build and Deliver

  • AI Opportunity & Workflow Audits: A candid walkthrough of your daily operations to find the two or three highest-return workflows worth automating first, and which ones should stay strictly human.
  • Custom Claude & Language-Model Internal Tools: Focused web utilities, document processors, research assistants, and prompt pipelines tailored to your company's voice, data structure, and approval steps.
  • Sentiment & Competitive Intelligence Pipelines: Automated systems that read buyer reviews, support notes, and competitor shifts so leadership sees recurring patterns without manually reading hundreds of rows.
  • Team Enablement & Usage Guardrails: Plain-English policies covering what company data can and cannot connect to personal or enterprise AI agents, paired with live working sessions for your staff.

How an AI Engagement Runs

Every engagement follows a transparent, no-lock-in rhythm. First, we define one concrete workflow problem and measure how long it takes today. Next, I architect and ship the working tool or workflow inside accounts you own, with mandatory human-in-the-loop approval gates on any external action. Finally, I train your team and hand over clean documentation so you are never trapped paying an agency to keep a script running.

Common Questions

How do you decide where AI is actually worth building?

I start by auditing where your team loses repeatable hours or misses critical insight in existing workflows. Every opportunity is ranked by practical business return, data readiness, and maintenance risk before writing a line of code.

Do we need a large engineering team to maintain the AI tools you build?

No. I design workflows to fit the tools your team already uses, keep dependencies minimal, and deliver plain-English documentation and live training so non-technical staff can run them safely.

How do you handle data privacy and human oversight in AI workflows?

Client data stays inside enterprise API tiers that do not train public models, and any workflow that sends external communication or updates records includes explicit human approval gates and clear access rules.

Can we start with a small AI audit before committing to a build?

Yes. Most clients start with a scoped AI workflow audit or a focused single-workflow build sprint so you see measurable time savings before expanding further.
