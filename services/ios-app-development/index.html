Common Questions

Why build natively in Swift and SwiftUI instead of a cross-platform wrapper?

Native SwiftUI apps feel immediately responsive on iPhone and iPad, adopt Apple platform capabilities cleanly, carry far fewer third-party dependency breaks during OS updates, and pass App Store review more predictably.

Who owns the Xcode source code and App Store account?

You do. Every project ships in your Git repository and publishes under your Apple Developer account so you retain 100% ownership of the codebase, signing certificates, and customer relationship.

Do you handle App Store review guidelines, privacy manifests, and StoreKit setup?

Yes. I handle StoreKit 2 product configuration, purchase restoration flows, Apple Privacy Manifests, support page setup, and App Review submission notes so launches do not stall in review.

What types of iOS apps are the best fit for Treanor Digital?

Focused utility apps, field-service and trade tools, exam prep and training apps, and companion mobile experiences where fast offline reliability and clean Apple design matter more than bloated social features.
