Common Questions

Do you build websites from scratch or work inside existing platforms like AEM and WordPress?

Both. For fast marketing sites I often build clean, zero-bloat static HTML and modern web stacks that load instantly and rank cleanly. For larger organizations, I advise on and architect inside existing enterprise platforms including Adobe Experience Manager (AEM).

What happens during a marketing technology stack audit?

I map every tool you pay for across CMS, analytics, CRM, email, and forms, identify overlapping subscriptions or broken data handoffs, and give you a plain-English simplification plan you can execute immediately.

Can you manage or evaluate an outside web agency on our behalf?

Yes. Because I started as a developer, I review vendor estimates, inspect actual code quality, catch bloated architectures early, and translate technical trade-offs into clear business choices.

What kind of LMS and training setups do you handle?

I design and configure structured learning portals and course architectures for customer education, trade certification prep, and internal team onboarding without over-engineering the platform.
