Background

I started my career writing code and moved into leading digital marketing, analytics, web architecture, and technology teams across both agency and enterprise environments. Over the past 20+ years, I have sat on both sides of the table: managing multi-brand digital programs and shipping the software underneath them.

That combination showed me a recurring problem for small and mid-sized businesses. Most companies are forced to split their work across separate vendors: a marketing agency that talks in impressions, a dev shop that waits for detailed tickets, and a stream of AI consultants who hand over slide decks nobody knows how to implement.

Treanor Digital LLC (originally established in 2018 and operating today as Treanor Digital in Monroe, Michigan) exists to close that gap. When you work with me, the person on the discovery call is the person auditing your site, shaping the plan, writing the code, and standing behind the result.

How I Work

  • Direct accountability: One point of contact from kickoff to launch, with no junior handoffs or agency account layers.
  • Evidence before retainers: Many engagements start with a Free Site & AI Search Report so you see concrete findings before committing budget.
  • Code you own: Every website, workflow, dashboard, or mobile app ships in your accounts with clear documentation and zero vendor lock-in.
  • Practical AI, not hype: I focus on AI tools and workflows that save measurable hours or sharpen buyer visibility across search and answer engines.

Core Capabilities

My work connects four areas that usually live in separate silos:

  • AI Strategy & Implementation: Workflow audits, custom Claude and language-model internal tools, sentiment and competitive tracking pipelines, and plain-English team training.
  • Digital Marketing & Search Visibility: Web UX and conversion strategy, traditional SEO paired with AEO and GEO (AI search visibility), paid acquisition, email lifecycle, and executive brand reporting.
  • Native iOS Engineering: End-to-end Swift and SwiftUI applications, offline-capable local storage, StoreKit 2 in-app purchase architecture, and App Store release management.
  • Web & Technology Consulting: Custom websites and applications, Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) and CMS architecture, marketing stack audits, LMS setup, and technical team direction.
